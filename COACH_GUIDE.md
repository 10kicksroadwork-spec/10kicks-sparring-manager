# Coach guide

Use this on your phone once the gym URL is live. Until that URL exists, these steps describe the app that is built. They are not a claim that production sign-in already works.

The match limits you see are proposed defaults: 15 lb **and** 10% of the lighter athlete, skill gap 1, and aggression gap 1. They are pending coach review and are not safety-certified. You still decide who should spar.

## Start Saturday

1. Sign in with the Google account an admin put on the coach list.
2. Tap **Start Saturday session**.
3. Check in each fighter and each volunteer. The boxes are large on purpose.
4. If someone walks in, type their name and weight, then tap **Add and check in**. They are saved on the roster.
5. Open **Session limits** only if you mean to change the numbers. You must tick the confirmation. That change is still not a safety certification.
6. Tap **Generate rundown**.

If a complete card is impossible, the screen says why and what you can do next, such as add a volunteer. It will not quietly drop a fighter to two sessions.

## Run the card

Each block has Main ring, Floor 1, and Floor 2. Fighters who are not listed in that block are on light/recovery and do not have an assigned partner.

- **Lock match** keeps that pairing when you rebuild the rest.
- **Mark block complete** means both rounds happened. An interruption does not count as a finished two-round session.
- **Regenerate unfinished** builds a new card and leaves completed and locked matches as they were.
- **Print** opens a black-on-white rundown you can print or share.

The revision number on the screen goes up after each save. If another coach saved first, refresh and try again. Your older screen will not overwrite theirs.

## Roster

Admins can deactivate a person. Deactivated people stay in old cards and do not get new ones. Named “do not pair” exclusions are also admin-only and cannot be waived with a numeric exception.
